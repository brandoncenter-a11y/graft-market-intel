## 4. Clinical & research

- **Yale/JCI study, published Aug 11, 2026:** "APOL1 risk alleles modulate T cell receptor signaling to promote allograft rejection," led by Madhav Menon, MD (Yale). The APOL1 G1 risk variant (linked to recent African ancestry) is associated with greater graft immune-cell infiltration, poorer graft survival, and T-cells that are relatively resistant to calcineurin-inhibitor suppression — recipients with the variant rejected despite higher tacrolimus levels, suggesting a need for alternative immunosuppression strategies in this population. ([JCI paper](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/193173) / [Medical Xpress](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-08-genetic-variants-kidney-transplant.html))
- Nothing new found in JASN, AJT, NEJM, or JAMA on kidney allocation, outcomes, or xenotransplantation dated in this window.
