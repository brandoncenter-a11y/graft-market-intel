## 3. Policy & regulatory

- **HHS/CMS began decertifying Network for Hope** (serves KY + parts of IN/OH/WV) — the second
  OPO decertification ever, after Life Alliance Organ Recovery Agency (Miami, Sept 2025). HRSA
  reviewed 351 authorized-but-not-completed donation cases from 2021–2024 and found at least 28
  patients may not have been deceased when organ procurement began. Network for Hope is appealing
  (15-business-day reconsideration window); its service area stays closed to competing OPOs while
  that's pending. This is the first real-world enforcement action since CMS proposed the
  CMS-3409-P OPO-tiering rule (DSA-level re-certification) in January — worth watching as an early
  signal of how aggressively CMS will apply that framework once finalized (still expected late
  2026; comment period closed Mar 31, nothing new on the final rule this week).
- **OPTN emergency policy shortens donor serology testing windows** — HIV testing window cut
  28→7 days, seasonal West Nile Virus window cut 14→7 days, effective **Aug 17, 2026**. This
  changes what transplant-programme and OPO backend software must track and enforce — a direct,
  dated compliance requirement landing in Graft's target segment.
- **No news this week** on the SRTR long-term-contract recompete (HHRI bridge still runs to
  Sep 18, 2026) or on a new OPTN public-comment cycle opening.

## 5. Metric watch

- **IOTA Performance Year 2 has 95 eligible kidney transplant hospitals**, not ~103 — CMS's own
  PY2 quick reference confirms the count after the low-volume threshold rose from 11 to 15
  transplants/yr (finalized Jun 1, already known; the resulting hospital count had not been
  reported until this week). Worth correcting internal materials and sales collateral that cite
  "~103 hospitals" for IOTA.
- The **OPTN/SRTR 2024 Annual Data Report** (34th annual report, national volume/waitlist/discard

## 6. So what for Graft

1. **CMS oversight now has a live enforcement case, not just a proposed rule.** Network for Hope
   is OPO-side (not Graft's segment directly), but it's the clearest evidence yet that
   CMS-3409-P-style tiering will be enforced with real teeth — strengthens the case that
   compliance/reporting burden is about to get heavier across the whole ecosystem, reinforcing
   Graft's hours-saved pitch to transplant programmes bracing for more oversight.
2. **The Aug 17 serology-window deadline is a concrete, dated pain point** every transplant
   programme and OPO must operationalize in the next week. It's a good example to have on hand in
   sales conversations of the kind of new tracking burden Graft's backend absorbs.
3. **NKR shipping ahead of a regulatory deadline is a competitive signal**, not just a feature
   note — it's more evidence NKR (already flagged as the roster's key competitor at the workflow
   layer) executes fast on infrastructure spanning both halves of Graft's funnel.
4. **Quiet capital markets** mean no new well-funded entrant to react to this week, but also no
   fresh signal to cite for fundraising narrative purposes.
