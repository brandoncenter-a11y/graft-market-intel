# Graft Transplant Weekly — 2026-08-06

*Exec one-pager. First issue — the market-intel repo was seeded and baselined earlier today, so
this week's "what changed" leans on that fresh baseline plus a same-day news sweep. Full detail
and sourcing lives in [`ecosystem-players.md`](../ecosystem-players.md).*

## 1. TL;DR

- **CareDx (top competitor) hit a 52-week high** (+12.8% this week) after a Q2 beat and a guidance
  raise to **$490–500M FY26**; its AlloSure/AlloMap Medicare coverage locks in **Aug 30** — more
  cash and validated recurring revenue funding the two products (TxAccess, AlloCare) that mirror
  Graft's own front-end + backend play.
- **OPTN's legacy-UNOS scope is now fully competed out**: HRSA awarded the last piece — Policy &
  Committee Support — to **Customer Value Partners** on Jul 27. The multi-vendor OPTN landscape
  Graft will need to interoperate with (GDIT+Arbor, Accenture/Leidos/Sapient, CVP) is largely set.
- **CMS's IOTA model got sharper teeth**: the PY2 final rule (effective Jun 1) raised the
  low-volume threshold 11→15 transplants/yr and folded in Medicare Advantage beneficiaries —
  a bigger financial incentive for the ~103 participating hospitals (Graft's buyer) to grow
  throughput and offer-acceptance.
- **InVita (OPO-side platform) acquired MAK-SYSTEM** (blood-management software, ex-Carlyle),
  closed Jun 30 — expands InVita into blood-bank workflows; stays OPO-side, not yet a
  centre-side (Graft-overlapping) threat.
- **Adjacent capital signal, not a near-term threat:** Gates/Bezos-backed LifeMine raised **$263M**
  (announced today) for LIFE-001, a next-gen immunosuppressant aimed partly at kidney transplant
  patients — kidney Phase 2 data isn't expected until 2028, but it shows real money still chasing
  the post-transplant-care problem space.

## 2. Market moves

- **CareDx** — Q2 2026 revenue $132M (+52% YoY), FY26 guidance raised to $490–500M; stock up
  ~13% this week on the print. AlloSure Kidney/AlloMap solid-organ molecular-testing LCD
  **finalized Jul 16**, effective **Aug 30, 2026** (up to 6 surveillance tests yr 1, 4/yr yrs 2–3).
  Reinforces CareDx as the single highest-priority company to monitor — it's the only player
  already shipping both a referral/donor-facing product (TxAccess) *and* a workup/waitlist
  backend (OTTR/TransChart/XynQAPI), i.e. both halves of Graft's stack.
- **InVita Healthcare Technologies (Novacap)** acquired **MAK-SYSTEM**, a global blood-management
  software leader, from The Carlyle Group (closed Jun 30). Combined entity now spans 30+
  countries, ~95% of US blood centres, ~75% of US deceased-organ donation. Primarily
  OPO/deceased-donation side — watch for any move toward centre-side (recipient workup) product.
- **No new funding rounds or raises found this week** for direct backend competitors (Afflo/
  Buckeye, OmniLife/FlowHawk, Lifefuser, Transplants.org) or front-end/donor-layer competitors
  (MatchingDonors.com, APKD, ATF). Somatus and Monogram Health still have no confirmed 2026
  primary round — don't cite the 2021 CapitalG/TPG figures still circulating in aggregator search
  results as current.
- **Adjacent:** LifeMine Therapeutics raised $263M ($75M Series D + $188M Series F, Gates/Bezos
  investors) to advance LIFE-001, a calcineurin-activation-inhibitor immunosuppressant, into
  Phase 2 in kidney transplant patients (data expected ~2028) and Phase 1b in islet-cell
  recipients. Not a workflow/software competitor — flagged because a successful next-gen
  immunosuppressant would eventually change post-transplant monitoring needs (relevant to
  CareDx's dd-cfDNA testing thesis more than to Graft directly).

## 3. Policy & regulatory

- **OPTN modernization: last legacy-UNOS task order awarded.** HRSA gave the **OPTN Policy
  Process & Committee Support Services** task order to **Customer Value Partners, LLC**
  (~$6.7M, 1-yr term) on **Jul 27, 2026**. Combined with the May 29 Patient Safety & Compliance
  award (GDIT + Arbor Research), the initial wave of OPTN scope-splitting is essentially
  complete. Next milestone to watch: the next-gen match/DonorNet replacement, anchored by
  GDIT + Accenture/Leidos/Sapient.
- **CMS IOTA model — PY2 final rule in effect (Jun 1, 2026).** Raised the low-volume-hospital
  threshold from 11 to 15 transplants/yr and added Medicare Advantage beneficiaries into the
  model's scope. IOTA remains a 6-year mandatory model (Jul 2025–Jun 2031) tying Medicare
  upside/downside risk payments at 103 hospitals to transplant-access and offer-acceptance
  metrics — the clearest financial tailwind in the market for coordinator-hours-saved tooling.
- **CMS OPO tiering rule (CMS-3409-P)** — proposed Jan 28, 2026 (DSA-level re-certification
  instead of OPO-level); comment period closed Mar 31. No final rule yet; still expected late
  2026. Nothing new to report this week.
- **SRTR contract recompete — no news this week.** HHRI's interim bridge contract runs to
  Sep 18, 2026 (options to Mar 2027) while HRSA runs the full competition. Worth a standing
  watch item since a new SRTR vendor could change the report-card metrics Graft's backend needs
  to support.
- **OPTN public comment cycle:** no new cycle opening this week; the most recent past cycle
  (Aug–Oct 2025) is closed. Watch for the next cycle announcement.

## 6. So what for Graft

1. **CareDx's cash position just got stronger and its coverage more locked-in** — expect
   continued investment in TxAccess/AlloCare (Graft's closest direct analog on both sides of the
   funnel). Track their product announcements and any centre-count claims for those two products
   specifically, not just the diagnostics business.
2. **IOTA's sharper incentives (higher low-volume threshold, MA inclusion) strengthen Graft's
   core pitch.** Consider building a simple IOTA-linked ROI calculator — coordinator hours saved
   → offer-acceptance/throughput lift → estimated IOTA payment impact — sized to the ~103
   participating hospitals, since that's a concrete, CMS-backed dollar figure sales can use.
3. **The OPTN vendor map is now largely settled** (GDIT+Arbor, Accenture/Leidos/Sapient, CVP).
   Worth a one-time technical scan of what interoperability/reporting requirements these vendors
   will impose on transplant-programme software (Graft's backend) as the next-gen match system
   comes online.
4. **InVita/MAK-SYSTEM and LifeMine are watch-only, not action items** — neither currently
   overlaps Graft's product surface (OPO-side and pharma respectively).
