## 3. Policy & regulatory

- **OPTN's emergency serology-testing policy took effect today, Aug 17, 2026.** Donor testing windows shorten from 14→7 days for seasonal West Nile Virus and 28→7 days for HIV, for all donors — a live data-capture and reporting change every backend transplant-programme and OPO system must now support. National Kidney Registry moved onto the new process a week early (Aug 10) via an expanded "Streamlined Serology Service" covering all 17 serology/crossmatch tests, to help member and non-member centres comply. ([HRSA policy notice, PDF](https://www.hrsa.gov/sites/default/files/hrsa/optn/seasonal-wnv-testing-required-for-all-donors.pdf) / [PR Newswire — NKR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/national-kidney-registry-ready-to-support-new-optn-seven-day-testing-requirements-302833572.html))
- **Network for Hope's decertification effective date is now confirmed as Aug 5, 2026** (one day before the Aug 6 refresh cutoff, which is why it was missed in that pass and only fully reconciled this week). HRSA's federal review found roughly 28 cases where organ procurement may have started on patients not yet deceased, with deficiencies found in about 30% of cases reviewed. Network for Hope is appealing; HRSA/CMS are overseeing the transition, and the Kentucky/Indiana/Ohio/West Virginia service area stays closed to competing OPOs until proceedings conclude. This is the first for-cause OPO decertification under the current federal enforcement push — a different mechanism from, but reinforcing, the still-pending CMS-3409-P OPO-tiering rule. ([HHS press release](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-decertifies-kentucky-organ-procurement-organization.html) / [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/hhs-decertify-organ-procurement-group-patient-safety-failures-network-for-hope/827334/))
- **No update this window** on CMS-3409-P (still proposed, final rule still expected late 2026), the SRTR long-term-contract recompete (HHRI bridge runs to Sep 18, 2026), or a live OPTN public-comment cycle. No new HRSA/SAM.gov OPTN-modernization task-order awards since the Jul 27 Customer Value Partners award.
